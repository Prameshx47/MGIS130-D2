Define your data sources

The calculator will use information entered by the user, including loan amount, loan term, credit score, state, lender type, interest rate, and any extra monthly payment. The app will also use loan settings and state benchmark rates stored in the code. These rates are sample values, not live rates from a bank or financial website.

Plan your processing

The app will use the loan amount, interest rate, and term to calculate the regular monthly payment. It will then create a payment schedule showing how much goes toward interest and principal each month. If the user adds extra payments, the app will update the payoff time, total interest, and interest saved. It will also update the results when the user changes their loan details.
Design your interface

Users will see a loan calculator with fields and sliders for the loan amount, term, credit score, interest rate, and extra payments. They can choose a loan type, state, and lender. The results area will show the monthly payment, total interest, total paid, payoff date, and payment schedule. The page will also include a chart, a button to export the schedule as a CSV file, and a theme switch. The layout will adjust for phones and larger screens.
